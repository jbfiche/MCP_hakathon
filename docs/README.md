## Planned, not yet written

The call promises learning materials two weeks before the event, covering three things. The
first is largely handled by [`plugins/`](../plugins/) and
[`inventory/`](../inventory/); these two remain:

- **`what-is-mcp.md`** — MCP for biologists, from scratch: what a host, client and server are;
  what tools, resources and prompts are and who chooses each; where MCP stops and Agent Skills
  start; and honestly, what agents cannot do for you. The audience is a clinician or a wet-lab
  biologist who has never written a client-server anything. This is the text behind the Day 1
  13:30–15:00 session.
- **`hackathon-day2.md`** — the working format: how to go from the Day 1 brainstorm to a demo by
  17:00, given that *every member of a group implements their own version of the server*. What to
  cut when you are behind, what to have ready for the demo, and how to publish afterwards.

*Organisers: both are content decisions rather than setup, so they are deliberately left for us
to draft together.*
